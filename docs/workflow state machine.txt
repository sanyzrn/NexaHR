## Verdict — 3 lines
The declarative `TRANSITIONS` machine itself is sound: all 18 transitions hold for the full chain, manager path and CEO-direct, both HTTP and copilot reach them through the same endpoint bodies, and the row-locking/finalize guards are real. The defects live where the machine meets the outside: the HR-shield collides with the departure/recovery flow to freeze `hr_subject` records permanently, the no-deputy chain shape has no CEO return, and three consumers (deadline notification, queue cards, employee stage label) have drifted from states the machine can actually reach. Everything below was executed, not inferred — 1035 backend tests, launcher, frontend and e2e all pass locally, and each finding was reproduced with a live probe against PostgreSQL 16.4.


## Findings — severity | file:line | what breaks | how to reach it | fix


**1. HIGH | app/api/routers/evaluations.py:1410 (also :1046, :1093, :1179, :1224) × app/api/routers/personnel.py:504–568 × app/services/notifications.py:234–238, app/services/scheduled.py:178–181 | The HR shield makes the documented recovery of an open `hr_subject` record impossible — the record freezes forever and the person becomes un-evaluatable.**
`ensure_hr_may_handle` 403s *every* HR user on every open record with `hr_review_skipped=True` (view, cancel, extend, claim, handover, reassign). But `_close_out_departure` only cancels the *departing* person's own record; for records where the leaver sits in a chain seat it just sends `seats_vacated`/`orphaned_case` notices instructing HR to «تغییر مسئول مرحله» or cancel. For `hr_subject` records both instructions return 403. Reproduced end-to-end: HR-unit member, supervisor+deputy chain → supervisor scores+submits (record `hr_approved`, awaiting deputy) → deputy departs via the standard personnel PATCH (200, vacated-seat notice sent) → HR `reassign` → 403, `cancel` → 403, even `GET` → 403; record frozen in `hr_approved`, `uq_open_evaluation_per_personnel` now blocks any replacement record for that person for life. Same shape via `ceo_submit_hr_subject` (CEO-direct + HR subject) when the CEO departs — the shape that has broken five times is the one with zero recovery. Note this is not the decided handover trade-off: the decided item was *who* may hand over; this is the recovery tools themselves being unreachable for a record class the system explicitly tells HR to recover. The 403 message («رسیدگی به آن با معاونت و مدیرعامل است») is also false in that state — the deputy is gone and the CEO has no legal move. **Fix:** split the guard into `may_view` vs `may_recover` and permit `cancel`/`reassign` (write-only, no read of scores/evidence) on shielded records, or auto-cancel a shielded record in `_close_out_departure` when the leaver is its only remaining actor.


**2. MEDIUM | app/services/workflow.py:299–307 (TRANSITIONS), consumed at app/api/routers/evaluations.py:960–1005 | In a no-deputy chain, the CEO cannot return a case from `hr_approved` — the final approver's only moves are rubber-stamp finalize or nothing.**
`ceo_return` starts from `deputy_approved`; `deputy_return` is dead in this shape (`deputy_user_id` is NULL, so the assignee check 403s everyone); `hr_return` only fires from `submitted`. So once HR approves, `hr_approved` has exactly one outbound edge: `ceo_finalize`. Reproduced: supervisor+CEO chain (deputy empty — the shape the importer documented as 9 of 42 real employees), run to `hr_approved`, CEO calls `/return` → 403 «این ارزیابی در مرحله تأیید نهایی توسط شما نیست», status unchanged. This contradicts the TRANSITIONS docstring's own rule («هر تأییدکننده می‌تواند پرونده را با ذکر دلیل یک مرحله عقب بفرستد») and mirrors the gap that `ceo_return_manager`/`ceo_return_ceo_only` were written to close. **Fix:** add `ceo_return_no_deputy`: `hr_approved → submitted`, `allowed_role=ceo`, `assignee_field="ceo_user_id"`, `guard=lambda r: r.deputy_user_id is None and not skips_hr_review(r)`, and map it in `return_evaluation` next to the other two CEO variants.


**3. MEDIUM | app/api/routers/evaluations.py:1142–1146 | Extending the submission window on a CEO-direct record notifies nobody — the one person who must submit never learns the deadline moved.**
`targets = [record.unit_supervisor_user_id or record.deputy_user_id]`; in CEO-direct both seats are NULL, so the extension succeeds but zero notifications are created. Reproduced: ceo-direct chain, open period with a past `ends_on`, record in `draft` → `extend-submission` returns 200, `submission_window_extended` notification table empty. The deadline was extended precisely so the CEO could submit; the extension is a column nobody sees until the next 400 on submit. **Fix:** derive the target from `scorer_seat(record)` (i.e., `scorer_field`) instead of the two middle seats — that already returns `ceo_user_id` for this shape.

MEDIUM-LOW | app/api/routers/dashboard.py:719–728 and :740–747; app/services/ai/tools/evaluations.py:494–506; app/schemas/evaluation.py:32–37 + frontend/src/components/WorkflowStepper.tsx:18–27 | Queue views and stage labels describe states the machine no longer has, and miss two states it does.**

**5. LOW | app/models/evaluation.py:242–245 and app/models/evaluation_access.py:43–46 vs alembic/versions/e9c47b3f1a52_distinct_chain_stages.py:59–69 | The models declare a CHECK constraint the migration deliberately does not create — a landmine against the legal single-decider chain that the schema-parity test cannot see.**
The migration creates only `*_supervisor_not_deputy` and `*_deputy_not_ceo` (its docstring: «مسئول واحد = مدیرعامل عمداً این‌جا نیست»), and `test_redundant_chain_seat.py` + `single_decider` pin supervisor==CEO as legal-and-disclosed. Verified in the live DB: `pg_constraint` has no such row, while `test_autogenerate_produces_no_changes` stays green — `compare_metadata` ignores CHECK constraints, so the test's core promise ("models and DB must agree") has exactly this hole. Concrete failure: any environment whose schema is (re)built or reconciled from model metadata, or a future migration written from the model declaration, bans the single-decider chain; the INSERT then dies in `create_evaluation`, whose `except IntegrityError` (app/api/routers/evaluations.py:451–468) masks it as a false 409 «برای این پرسنل یک ارزیابی باز وجود دارد» — a misleading error pointing at the wrong invariant. **Fix:** delete the two phantom declarations; if you want real insurance, make the parity test compare CHECK constraints explicitly (they are the one class `compare_metadata` skips).


## Verified correct
- **The transition table itself.** I traced all 18 transitions against every legal shape (full, manager, CEO-direct, no-deputy, `hr_subject` variants of each, and the CEO-as-supervisor single-decider chain): every reachable state has a forward edge, every return lands where an actor exists, and the `is_manager_path`/`is_ceo_only_path`/`skips_hr_review` guards correctly partition the four submit variants, the two HR-approve variants, and the four CEO-return variants. The `ceo_finalize` guard (`status is not hr_approved OR deputy is None`) is exactly right: it allows the no-deputy shortcut while blocking deputy-skip in full chains (pinned by `test_the_ceo_cannot_finalize_before_the_deputy`).
- **Both paths per guard (rule 3).** Every copilot workflow action (`_ADVANCE`, ai/tools/evaluations.py:287–297) delegates to the real endpoint function, and its role gate exactly replicates the router's `Depends` (`_stage` ≙ `require_chain_stage`, `_exact` ≙ `require_roles`, verified action-by-action; `test_ai_workflow_parity` enforces the enum match). `ensure_hr_may_handle` sits inside `apply_transition` and `ensure_module_enabled` stays deliberately in endpoint bodies — the copilot cannot bypass either.
- **The finalize belt-and-suspenders.** `apply_transition` refuses finalize without computed scores (count check before `before()`) and without a snapshot (after `before()`), so an un-scored or un-snapshotted record cannot be closed from either path; the copilot's `ceo_finalize` builds snapshot + verify_token and runs the archive task inline. Concurrent transitions are serialized by `with_for_update(of=EvaluationRecord)` on every state-changing route including `upsert_scores` and `evaluator-comment`.
- **Paired invariants (rule 4) — the ones that hold:** `OPEN_STATUSES`/`IS_OPEN_RECORD` agree; `hr_panel_is_shielded`/`IS_SHIELDED_FROM_HR_PANEL` are character-for-character the same rule; backend `compute_result` vs frontend `computePreview` use identical rounding, present-section re-normalization and zero-weight fallback, with bonus excluded on both sides; every `suppressed_avg` call site in reports/analytics/dashboard pairs with `cohort_size` (people, not rows); the partial unique index `uq_open_evaluation_per_personnel` exists, is UNIQUE and partial, and its race window is closed by the index plus winner re-fetch.
- **Window semantics.** The deadline binds only scoring/self-assessment (deliberately not the approval stages), extension is date-based and can only lengthen, extension on a period-less record is refused with a 400 (probe-verified), and records without a period stay unlimited.
- **Cancellation, claim, handover, reassign guards** (outside the shielded-record hole in finding 1): mandatory reasons land in both comment and audit within the transaction, handover validates target role/active/subject, reassign refuses empty seats (shape preserved), duplicate seats across stages, and subject-as-evaluator.
- **Notifications for workflow actions** match every shape (`owner_after_hr_review`, `scorer_seat`, `_tell_the_scorer` self-notify suppression, `_hr_queue_ids` subject exclusion), and the SLA/orphan sweeps derive owners from chain shape, not status alone.
- **The public verify endpoint**: random unguessable token, finalized-only, rate-limited, minimal payload.
